## One-liner

MemoryPlace is a lightweight, aesthetic mnemonic training simulator where users design a spatial memory palace in 2D, walk it in 3D, place loci on walls, and practice recall through spatial navigation and flashcards.

## Problem

Learners struggle to retain information because most study tools are abstract, flat, and disconnected from how human memory naturally works.
Classical memory palaces solve this through spatial encoding, but building and practicing them is difficult without guidance.

MemoryPlace solves this by giving users a clean, simple, mentally reconstructible palace builder and a first‑person training environment that teaches the real mnemonic technique.

## Who it's for

Primary user: individuals who want to practice and master the memory palace technique — students, autodidacts, professionals, and memory athletes.

Secondary user: instructors or coaches who want to provide structured palace layouts or loci sequences.

## Core loop

1. Design  
   Build rectangular rooms in a clean 2D blueprint.
   Add doors or archways.
   Place loci on walls.

2. Populate  
   Attach flashcards or mnemonic cues to loci.
   Choose floating icons or plaques.

3. Navigate  
   Walk the palace in first‑person 3D.
   Visit loci in spatial order.
   Strengthen recall through embodied navigation.

4. Reinforce  
   Run quizzes tied to loci.
   Review cards in spatial context.
   Practice until the palace is mentally reconstructible.

## End state

In the finished version, users will have:

A primary palace with themed rooms designed for daily mnemonic practice

Optional additional palaces for specialized subjects or guided cognitive workflows

A clean 2D builder for designing rectangular rooms with doors or archways

A first‑person 3D environment for embodied spatial navigation

Wall‑anchored loci that hold flashcards, mnemonic cues, and imagery

A quiz system that reinforces recall through spatial traversal

A palace layout that can be mentally reconstructed and used outside the app

A lightweight, aesthetic interface that teaches real memory palace techniqu

## Non-goals

MemoryPlace is not:

A general flashcard app

A note-taking platform

A social network

A multiplayer or collaborative builder

A realistic 3D simulation with complex geometry

A mobile-first experience (initially)
